Business: Banner Heating and Air Conditioning Company, Van Nuys, CA. HVAC.

1. Current state audit

Every fact below is documented in the pool file or visible on a public page named next to it. Nothing else is assumed.

1

The Google profile is unclaimed. Birdeye states on the profile page: "This profile has not been claimed by the business owner or representative." (Birdeye profile page, also recorded in the pool file.) An unclaimed profile means the owner cannot edit it, reply to reviews, or correct wrong information, and anyone can suggest edits to it.

2

Rating and reviews: 5.0 stars with 44 reviews. (Birdeye profile page. BestProsInTown also shows 5 out of 5, per the pool file.) The reputation is excellent. The profile around it is not.

3

Address and phone on the listing: 14717 1/2 Oxnard St, Van Nuys, CA 91411, phone (818) 988-7101. (Birdeye profile page, matches the website contact block.)

4

Hours conflict. The listing shows Monday to Friday 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. and Saturday and Sunday 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. (Birdeye profile page.) The company website advertises "24/7 Emergency Service" and "available 24/7 for your emergency needs." (banneraircon.com homepage.) A customer reading the Google profile sees a shop that closes at 5, while the shop itself says it answers around the clock. Both cannot be the first impression.

(banneraircon.com homepage, also recorded in the pool file.) The profile description says "over 30 years," which undersells a 1951 start by decades.

The contact email is bannerair@sbcglobal.net. (banneraircon.com homepage, also recorded in the pool file.)

Small crew, owner in the field. The pool file records that the owner, Abbey, works in the field with a small crew, and one review says he "came out the same day with a team of three technicians." (Pool file, from the business reviews.) This is a hands on local shop, which is exactly the kind of business a well kept profile helps.

2. The fix list

Claim and verify the profile

  • Claim the Google Business Profile and complete verification, so the owner controls the name, hours, phone, photos, and review replies.
  • Confirm the business name, address, and phone match the website and invoices exactly: Banner Heating and Air Conditioning Company, 14717 1/2 Oxnard St, Van Nuys, CA 91411, (818) 988-7101.
  • Add the owner as the primary owner and one backup manager account so the profile is never locked to one login.

Categories

  • Primary category: HVAC contractor.
  • Secondary categories: Air conditioning contractor. Heating contractor. Air conditioning repair service. Furnace repair service.
  • One primary category keeps the profile focused on the searches that bring in jobs, and the secondary categories cover the repair and heating searches the shop already earns reviews for.

Services list

Each service gets one plain sentence. All services below come from the services the company already lists on its own website.

Air conditioning repair

Diagnosis and repair of home and business cooling systems that are blowing warm, leaking, or not starting.

Air conditioning installation

New system installation and replacement, including Trane equipment, with free in home estimates.

Heating and furnace repair

Repair of furnaces and heating systems that will not start, cycle wrong, or heat unevenly.

Heat pump service

Installation, repair, and maintenance of heat pump systems.

Wall heater service

Repair and replacement of wall heaters.

Maintenance contracts

Scheduled seasonal service visits that keep heating and cooling systems checked before the heavy use months.

Custom engineering and design

Designed systems for homes and commercial spaces, including cooling for studios and computer rooms.

Emergency service

Around the clock emergency heating and cooling calls, matching the 24/7 service the company already advertises on its website.

Hours

  • Confirm the real hours with the owner first, then publish one consistent set on the profile, the website, and every directory.
  • Working set to confirm: Monday to Friday 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Saturday and Sunday 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m., as the listing shows today.
  • State the emergency line clearly in the description and a profile post, so the 24/7 emergency service the website promises is visible on Google too instead of contradicting the posted hours.
  • Add holiday hours for Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year each season, so customers never find a wrong open or closed status.

Attributes

  • Set every factual attribute Google offers for this business type, and only the ones that are true.
  • Mark that the business offers free in home estimates and takes online service requests, both of which the website already states.
  • Mark service options that apply, such as onsite services, once the owner confirms them.
  • Skip any attribute that cannot be backed up. Attributes are facts, not advertising.

Business description, rewritten

The current description leans on award years from 2017 to 2019 and undersells the company history. Replacement description, written from documented facts only:

"Banner Heating and Air Conditioning has served homes and businesses in the San Fernando Valley and West Los Angeles since 1951. The company installs, repairs, and maintains heating and cooling systems for residential, commercial, and industrial customers. Services include heat pumps, wall heaters, maintenance contracts, and custom engineering and design, including cooling for studios and computer rooms. Banner is a certified Trane Comfort Specialist dealer and offers free in home estimates. Emergency service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. The company is fully insured and guarantees its HVAC work. Call (818) 988-7101 for service or an estimate."

Every claim in that paragraph is on the company website today: the 1951 start, the service area, the Trane Comfort Specialist dealer status, free in home estimates, 24/7 emergency service, fully insured, and the work guarantee.

Photo plan

Eighteen photos, all shot on a phone, no photographer and no stock images.

  • 1 cover photo: the storefront or the service truck with the company name readable.
  • 2 exterior shots: the Oxnard St location from the street and the entrance.
  • 3 team shots: the owner and technicians in uniform, one group photo and two working shots.
  • 4 job shots: a technician servicing an outdoor unit, a furnace check, a thermostat install, and tools laid out on a real job.
  • 3 equipment shots: a new Trane install before and after, and one clean finished install close up.
  • 2 vehicle shots: the truck parked at a customer home and the truck loaded for the day.
  • 2 office shots: the dispatch desk and the phones, so the profile feels like a real working shop.
  • 1 logo photo: the company logo on a clean background for the profile logo slot.
  • Rule for every photo: real jobs, real people, taken in daylight where possible, and no customer faces or house numbers without permission.

Review routine

The shop already earns 5 star reviews. It has no system asking for them, which is why 44 reviews is a small number for a company trading since 1951.

  • One text message, sent after every completed job, to every customer, happy or not. No screening, no incentives, no discounts for reviews, which keeps the routine inside Google review policy.
  • The tech or the office sends it the same day, while the visit is fresh.
  • One follow up only if there is a problem, and that is a service call, not a second review ask. Review replies are handled under the monthly plan in section 4.

Exact text message, with the profile review link dropped in at send time:

"Hi, this is Banner Heating and Air Conditioning. Thanks for having us out today. If you have a minute, a Google review helps a local shop like ours more than any ad. Here is the link: [review link]. Thanks again for your business."
